It's like me and Mr Ritchie are on the same wavelength, as I was getting a hankering for some new stuff. So much so that I had some content planned for him on the site. Genre bending check, some great vocals from the man himself. I'm just a massive fan of his work.

Him (Greyworm in Game of Thrones) and Childish Gambino. (Troy in Community, because yeah I've been listening to him for ages, way before Because the Internet came out.
